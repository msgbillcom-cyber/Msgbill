## Google AdSense Requirements Checklist

- [x] **Privacy Policy** – Must be visible (you have /privacy)
- [x] **Original Content** – No copied content; write fresh
- [ ] **15-20+ Quality Posts** – Aim for 1000+ words each
- [ ] **Sufficient Traffic** – Build organic search traffic over 2-3 months
- [ ] **Good UX** – Fast load, mobile-friendly, clear navigation
- [ ] **No Policy Violations** – No adult content, violence, hate speech
- [ ] **About/Contact** – Clear info about the site and publisher

## Content Template for AdSense-Friendly Posts

1. **Title**: Include target keyword + year (e.g., "2026")
2. **Length**: 1000-1500 words minimum
3. **Structure**:
   - H1: Main title (with keyword)
   - Intro: 150-200 words, hook + what reader will learn
   - H2 sections: 4-6 subsections
   - H3 where needed
   - Bullet points, numbered lists
   - FAQ section (helps with featured snippets)
   - Conclusion + CTA
4. **CTA**: Soft MsgBill mention, not aggressive
5. **Images**: Add alt text, compress for speed
6. **Internal links**: Link to 2-3 related blog posts

---

## Publishing Cadence

- **Week 1-2**: 1 post/day (build momentum)
- **Week 3-4**: 5-6 posts/week (sustain)
- **Month 2+**: 3-4 posts/week (maintain + update old posts)

---

## AdSense Approval Timeline

- Typically 1-4 weeks after applying
- Need consistent traffic (even 100-500/day helps)
- Quality > quantity; one great post beats five thin ones

3. Add `/blog/[slug]` to sitemap (dynamic)
4. Submit sitemap to Google Search Console
5. Apply for AdSense once you have 15+ posts and some traffic
