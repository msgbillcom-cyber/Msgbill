Use any tool - Excel, Word, Google Docs, or [MsgBill](https://invoiceease.in)

### Step 2: Save as PDF

**In Excel:**

**In Word:**

1. File → Export
2. Create PDF/XPS Document
3. Save

1. Open WhatsApp on your phone
2. Find client's chat
3. Click paperclip/attachment icon (📎)

   Thank you! 🙏"_

## Best Practices for WhatsApp Invoicing

### ✅ DO:

1. **Send during business hours** (10 AM - 6 PM)
   - Higher open rates
   - Faster response

2. **Include multiple payment methods**
   - UPI (most popular)
   - Bank transfer (NEFT/IMPS)
   - Payment link
   - QR code

3. **Use professional format**
   - Your logo
   - GST details
   - Clear terms
   - Thank you note

4. **Follow up politely**
   - After 2 days: Gentle reminder
   - After 5 days: Payment confirmation request
   - After 7 days: Call if needed

5. **Personalize the message**
   - Use client's name
   - Reference the service/product
   - Sound friendly, not robotic

### ❌ DON'T:

1. **Send huge files**
   - Keep PDFs under 2MB
   - Compress if needed

2. **Spam with reminders**
   - Max 2-3 follow-ups
   - Space them out (2-3 days apart)

3. **Use unprofessional language**
   - Avoid "URGENT", "PAY NOW"
   - Be polite and professional

4. **Forget GST details**
   - GSTIN required if registered
   - Tax breakdown mandatory
   - HSN codes for items

5. **Skip terms & conditions**
   - Payment due date
   - Late payment penalties
   - Return/refund policy

## FAQ

### Q: Is it professional to send invoices on WhatsApp?

**A:** Absolutely YES! WhatsApp is now widely accepted for B2B and B2C
communication in India. As long as your invoice is properly formatted with all
legal requirements (GST details, terms), it's completely professional.

### Q: Can I track if my client opened the invoice?

**A:**

- **WhatsApp:** Shows "read" receipts (blue ticks)
- **MsgBill:** Advanced tracking shows exact view time + duration

### Q: What about GST compliance?

**A:** Your invoice must still have all GST-mandated fields:

- Your GSTIN
- Client GSTIN (if registered)
- Tax breakdown (CGST/SGST or IGST)
- HSN/SAC codes
- Place of supply

MsgBill calculates all this automatically.

### Q: Is WhatsApp safe for business documents?

**A:** Yes! WhatsApp uses end-to-end encryption. Only you and your client can
read messages. However:

- Don't share sensitive bank details
- Use payment links instead

### Q: Can I automate WhatsApp invoicing?

**A:** Yes, with tools like MsgBill:

- Auto-send after service completion
- Automated reminders
- Payment confirmation notifications
